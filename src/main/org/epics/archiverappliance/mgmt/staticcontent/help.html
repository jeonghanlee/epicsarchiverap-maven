Using the console

What each button and list of the page in front of you does. The full manual is under More.

Moving around

The menu bar
Choose a task: Archive PVs, Browse PVs, System, Help, Analyzer. The entry of the page you are on is underlined.
Menu button
On a narrow screen the entries fold behind it. Tap it to open the list, tap an entry to go there.
The address
The Browse PVs page keeps what you selected and the window in its address. Copy the address or bookmark it to come back to the same view.

Archive PVs

Add PVs to the archiver and follow them until they are archived.

Add one PV or a list

  1. Type one name, or paste a list with one name per line, into Add PVs. The note under the box counts the names and points at any that are not valid PV names.
  2. Leave Sampling as it is to let the policy of the site choose the method and the period of each PV. Open it to choose Monitor or Scan, a period, a policy or a controlling PV for every name.
  3. Click Archive. Each name appears under Requested in this visit with the answer of the request and its state.

Follow the requests

  1. The state goes from Appliance assigned and Initial sampling to Being archived, which takes a few minutes. The row then shows how the PV is sampled and by which policy.
  2. A name that no server answers stays in Initial sampling. Click Cancel request in its row.
  3. A PV that is being archived has Pause and Stop archiving…; a paused one has Resume. Select several rows to act on them together; Cancel requests acts on the ones that are waiting.

To look at the data of an archived PV, open it in Browse PVs.

Browse PVs

This entry opens the Browse PVs page: Find on the left, the chart and the table of the PVs you selected on the right. To add PVs, use Archive PVs; the link under Find opens it with the name you typed.

Find a PV

  1. Type in Find. The list below shows the archived PVs that match as you type.
  2. Choose how the text matches: Contains (words in any order, such as bpm 02 x), Glob (the whole name with * and ?, such as SR:BPM:0?:X) or Regex (a regular expression, such as CAV[12]:TEMP). The i button beside the three modes explains the modes, Show and the prefix buttons.
  3. To narrow further, pick a list in Show (for example Disconnected now) or press a prefix button under it; press the same button again to show every PV again.

Select PVs

  1. Click a name in the list to select it; click again to clear it. With the keyboard: arrow keys to move, Space or Enter to select. Under the count, Selected only shows only the selected PVs in the list until you press it again, and Clear selection clears every selection.
  2. Each selected PV gets a row in the table (up to 100) with its protocol, state, trend, value and the time of its last event. The i button beside the count of the table explains the columns; the one in the State column says what a lost sample is and what to do about it.
  3. Turn on Plot in a row to draw that PV in the chart (up to 5 at a time). When 5 are on, the other Plot boxes are disabled until you turn one off; point at one, or tap it, to read why. First 5, beside the count of the table, turns on the first five rows and turns off the others; Deselect all turns every Plot box off; Plotted only shows only the rows whose Plot box is on, until you press it again; Clear selection unticks every PV, so the table is empty.
  4. Click Details in a row to see what the archiver reports about the PV, in groups.

Look at another time window

  1. Click 10m, 1h, 6h, 24h or 7d above the chart.
  2. Drag across the chart to look at that part only. In the line above the chart, Earlier and Later move the window, also before you drag, and Back to the selected range returns. Value and Last event in the table then show the newest sample at or before the end of the window.
  3. Hover over the chart to read the values at a time.
  4. Beside the range buttons, Refresh chooses how often the page reads again: Off, 10 s, 30 s or 1 min. Refresh now reads once, and Updated gives the time of the last read. A window that ends earlier than now is not refreshed.

Pause, resume or stop a PV

  1. Use the buttons in the row of the PV. Only the buttons that fit its state appear: Pause or Resume, More, Cancel request for a request that has not started, or a link to Archive PVs for a PV that is not archived.
  2. More holds Change sampling…, Maintenance… (rename, type change and consolidation; its i button explains the three) and Stop archiving…. Each says what it does before it acts.
  3. To act on every PV of the table at once, use Pause all selected…, Resume all selected… or Stop archiving all selected… under the table. Each asks before it acts.

Open PVs in the Analyzer

Click Open in the analyzer above the chart to open the plotted PVs, or Open all selected in the analyzer under the table to open every PV of the table. The analyzer opens with the same PVs and window.

System

  1. Read the first lines of Overview: storing samples, reading data and room left, each Normal, Attention, Problem or Not reported.
  2. For a measure that is not Normal, read its Detail and click the link in Open. It opens the part of Operations or Retrieval performance that shows the measures behind it.
  3. Every i button of the console opens the same kind of card: a question as its title, In short as the answer, then what the item shows, when it is Normal, needs Attention or is a Problem, and What to do. A card of a control explains its items instead.
  4. The tabs Overview, Operations, Retrieval performance and External servers switch between the pages. When there are several appliances, a list beside the tabs chooses which one you look at.
  5. In Operations, each section answers the question under its heading; the i beside the heading says what the section shows, when it needs attention and what to do, and the small i beside a row says in one sentence what that value is. The treemap at the end of Stores shows which name prefixes use the space; click one to find its PVs in Browse PVs. The table of disconnected PVs by host has PVs to list them, Open in Browse PVs to select them on the Browse PVs page, and Pause these….
  6. A chart on Operations or Retrieval performance has Options, to change the line, the scale and the marks, and Enlarge chart. Your choice stays on the page until you leave it.
  7. In Retrieval performance, the i button beside a heading says what is slow and what to do. Type a PV, choose a range and click Measure to time a data request from this browser; Look up shows the stores and the state of one PV.
  8. In External servers, click Add a server…, give the retrieval address of another EPICS Archiver Appliance cluster and click Connect to serve its data from this appliance too. Remove… beside a server stops that. The i buttons beside the heading and in the two windows say what the address is and what a removal changes.

Analyzer

  1. Type an archived PV in Add a PV; names that match appear as you type. Click Add to plot it. This does not start archiving.
  2. Click remove beside a PV to take it off the chart.
  3. Click 10m to 7d, or set From and To and click Show, to choose the time window.
  4. Copy link copies an address that opens the same view; Download CSV saves the data shown.
