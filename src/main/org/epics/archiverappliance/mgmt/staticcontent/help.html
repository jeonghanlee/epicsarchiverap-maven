Moving around

The menu bar
Choose a task: Archive PVs, Browse PVs, System, Help, Analyzer. The entry of the page you are on is underlined.
Menu button
On a narrow screen the entries fold behind it. Tap it to open the list, tap an entry to go there.
The address
The Work page keeps what you selected and the window in its address. Copy the address or bookmark it to come back to the same view.

Archive PVs and Browse PVs

Both entries open the Work page: Find on the left, the PVs you selected on the right, their detail at the bottom.

Find a PV

  1. Type in Find. The list below shows the archived PVs that match as you type.
  2. Choose how the text matches: Contains (words in any order, such as bpm 02 x), Glob (the whole name with * and ?, such as SR:BPM:0?:X) or Regex (a regular expression, such as CAV[12]:TEMP).
  3. To narrow further, pick a list in Show (for example Disconnected now) or tap a name group under it.

Select PVs

  1. Click a name in the list to select it; click again to clear it. With the keyboard: arrow keys to move, Space or Enter to select.
  2. Selected PVs appear in Scan (up to 100) and in the chart (up to 5).
  3. Click a row of Scan to bring that PV to the front: the chart highlights it and Detail shows it.

Archive a new PV

  1. Type the full name in Find. When no archived PV has it, a button Archive name appears under the count.
  2. Click it, choose Monitor or Scan and the period, and click Archive.
  3. The PV joins Scan; its State turns to Being archived when the engine reaches it. If it stays in Initial sampling, the name may be wrong: open its Detail and click Abort the request.

Look at another time window

  1. Click 10m, 1h, 6h, 24h or 7d above Scan.
  2. Drag across the chart to look at that part only. Earlier and Later then move the window, Back to the selected range returns.
  3. Hover over the chart to read the values at a time.

Pause, resume or stop a PV

  1. Click the PV in Scan and go to Detail. Only the buttons that fit its state appear: Pause or Resume, Change sampling…, Stop archiving…, or Abort the request for a request that has not started.
  2. Rename, type change and consolidation are under Maintenance; everything the archiver knows about the PV is under All details.
  3. To act on every selected PV at once, use Pause all selected…, Resume all selected… or Stop archiving all selected… under Scan. Each asks before it acts.

Open PVs in the Analyzer

Click Open all selected in the viewer under Scan, or Open in the viewer in Detail. The viewer opens with the same PVs and window.

System

  1. Read the first lines of Overview: storing samples, reading data and room left, each Normal, Attention, Problem or Not reported.
  2. For a measure that is not Normal, click its link in the Detail column. It opens the part of Operations or Retrieval performance that explains it and says what to change.
  3. The tabs Overview, Operations, Retrieval performance and External servers switch between the pages. When there are several appliances, a list beside the tabs chooses which one you look at.
  4. In Operations, the i button beside a section heading says when that section needs attention and what to do. The table of disconnected PVs by host has PVs to list them, Open in Work to select them on the Work page, and Pause these….
  5. In Retrieval performance, type a PV, choose a range and click Measure to time a data request from this browser; Look up shows the stores and the state of one PV.
  6. In External servers, click Add a server…, give the retrieval address of another EPICS Archiver Appliance cluster and click Connect to serve its data from this appliance too. Remove… beside a server stops that.

Analyzer

  1. Type an archived PV in Add a PV; names that match appear as you type. Click Add to plot it. This does not start archiving.
  2. Click remove beside a PV to take it off the chart.
  3. Click 10m to 7d, or set From and To and click Show, to choose the time window.
  4. Copy link copies an address that opens the same view; Download CSV saves the data shown.
